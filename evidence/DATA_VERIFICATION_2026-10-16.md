# Data verification of the tender-offer panel (2026-10-16, Stage 1 of the JFS re-edit)

## Method
1. All 143 filings referenced by the panel (130 in `m2_tender_panel_v2.json` + 13 Q3-2026 filings from `q3_monitor_log.json`) were downloaded from EDGAR into `results/filings_cache/<FUND>_<date>_<accession>/` (primary document + exhibits; 0 errors). Every later check runs offline on these documents.
2. A second extractor written independently of `m2_main_v2.py` (`code/build_panel_v3.py`: sentence segmentation, share-class sums with decimals, dollar amounts and years excluded, footnotes excluded, explicit "accepted for purchase 100%" / "purchased all validly tendered" / payment-for-all-tendered-shares rules, expiry-date parsing) produced `results/m2_tender_panel_v3.json`, with the evidence sentence stored for every value.
3. `code/build_quarterly_v3.py` assigns each filing to a fund-quarter by the quarter of (offer expiry − 15 days), keeps the final filing per fund-quarter (corrected re-filings and final results supersede interim estimates), and computes fill rate, oversubscription, unfilled carry-over, the new-demand floor and the count of gated quarters → `results/m2_quarterly_v3.json` (108 fund-quarters).

## Agreement with the submitted panel
- Tendered shares: 128 of 130 rows identical (two differences are additions: OSCF 2025-12-23 ≈8.3M and 2026-03-27 ≈13.9M are interim estimates "approximately X million Shares", superseded by the final filings 2026-02-17 and 2026-05-15).
- Accepted shares: 94 rows differ, all in one direction — v2 recorded `None` (or a footnote amount) where the filing states full acceptance. v3 sets accepted = tendered when the filing says "accepted for purchase 100% of the Shares … validly tendered" (51 rows), "purchased all validly tendered … Shares" (OCIC/OTIC), or pays "the net asset value of the total amount of the Shares tendered" with no pro-rata language (43 rows, flagged `full_fill_by_payment`). The four BCRED "accepted" values in v2 (2,312; 2,912; 32,840; 10,247) were footnote amounts (shares of a prior offer repurchased at an earlier NAV); the analysis had dropped them as artifacts, and the correct values are full acceptance.

## Field definitions (from the filing language; recorded here so no value is a guess)
- *Tendered*: "Shares of the Fund were validly tendered and not withdrawn prior to the expiration of the Offer" (SC TO-I/A, final amendment, item 2); multi-class filings list classes separately and are summed. Fractional shares occur (OSCF, PCAP, ASIF).
- *Accepted*: "accepted for purchase <n> Shares on a pro rata basis based on the number of tendered Shares" when oversubscribed; "accepted for purchase 100% of the Shares" / "purchased all validly tendered … Shares" when not. Payment statements ("paid … representing the net asset value of the total amount of the Shares tendered") imply full acceptance; v3 flags these separately.
- *Cap*: "offered to repurchase up to 5% of shares outstanding" (fund-specific wording; BCRED and OSCF increased the cap under Rule 13e-4(f)(1) in 2026).
- *Quarter*: calendar quarter of (expiry − 15 days); each fund's cycle is its own, so cross-fund quarters are approximate alignments, which the manuscript must state.

## Census, part 2 (16:40) — timing of the "proration news" classification
`m2_revision2.py` classifies a fund-quarter as post-news when the **results filing date** is on or after 2026-03-27 (ASIF's first proration disclosure). Requests, however, are submitted before the offer **expires**, and results are filed 4–8 weeks later. With expiry dates now parsed from every filing, seven 2026Q1 fund-quarters are misclassified: ADS (expired 2026-03-16), ASIF (03-20), BCRED (03-02), FPCF (02-27), HLEND (03-04), OSCF (03-16) and PCAP (03-27) closed **before** any proration news and were coded post-news. These include the largest first jumps in the sample (ASIF 21.6M → 45.3M, HLEND 18.9M → 45.6M, ADS 28.2M → 66.9M, BCRED 85.0M → 133.7M), which therefore preceded gate news and coincided with the March 2026 market stress (the dates used in the pilot event study: 11 and 18 March). Only OCIC and OTIC (expiry 2026-03-31, windows open on 03-27) were exposed to the news within their Q1 window. Consequence: the Table 6 ProrationNews coefficient mixes pre-news and post-news request growth; the regression must be re-estimated with post defined by expiry (or by the fund's own first proration), and the narrative must separate the pre-news Q1 surge (valuation/stress channel) from the post-news Q2 increases in gated funds (the queue channel). Twin-discount alignment uses the same filing-date anchor and must be re-anchored to the tender window.

Event dates (Table 7): for ASIF (03-27), OCIC/OTIC (04-27), GCRED (05-14) and BCRED (06-04) no earlier current-quarter proration disclosure exists in 8-K/424B3 filings of the prior 45 days (checked 2026-10-16); the 8-K/424B3 hits are boilerplate repurchase-program language or an unrelated notes exchange offer (GCRED). BCRED's 2026-04-29 8-K states that "including capital invested by Blackstone and its senior leaders, BCRED was able to meet all repurchase requests for the quarter", confirming full acceptance of the March offer with sponsor support.
